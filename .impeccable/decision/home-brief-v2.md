# Home — qbitlab.tech (v2)

Scope: the single-page official site (home route). Visitor mode: **Persuade**.
Audience: non-technical SMB owners. Action: book a call, send an inquiry, or message on WhatsApp.
Proof: named past projects only (draft-gated). Photography is licensed stock (Pexels) illustrating each service — never presented as clients.
Constraints: static export; contact values in `src/content/site.json`; media provenance in `src/content/media.ts`.
Direction is brief-pinned by the user (2026-09-17): Apple AirPods Pro product page as the reference, and a "Canvas Scroll Clip" image-sequence scroll animation — not a live 3D model. v1 (design annual + live qubit) was rejected and is archived in `.archive/v1-design-annual`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: QBitLab presented like an Apple product page: a pinned black chapter where a real image sequence of a processor scrubs with the scroll while the promise appears line by line, then bright chapters where every service is shown in large photography. It refuses the generic agency template of icon-card grids and stock hero gradients.

OWN-WORLD: Apple product-page grammar. White #FFFFFF and #F5F5F7 fields with one pure #000 chapter; ink #1D1D1F, secondary gray #6E6E73; a blue #0071E3 pill for every primary action. One neo-grotesque (Inter) at 600 for giant centered headlines with tight tracking and at 400 for 17–21px body; small chapter titles above statements; rounded 28px photo cards; a frosted sticky local nav.

STORY: An SMB owner watches the processor come alive on black as the page promises answered customers, picked-up calls, and moved data. They scroll through three chapters — conversations, automation, websites & apps — finding their own problem next to a photo of it. They see how a project runs, then book a call, send an inquiry, or message on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A frosted 52px local nav: QBitLab left, section links, blue "Book a call" pill right. Below it a full-bleed black canvas showing frame 1 of the processor sequence, with "QBitLab" small and "AI that does the busywork." as a large white centered headline and a scroll cue beneath. Signature interaction: the section pins for about three viewports while 120 frames scrub with the scroll; the headline hands off to three statements in turn and the last reveals a Book a call button. Motion grammar: scroll-scrubbed image sequence, fade-up headlines once per chapter, photo cards easing from 92% to full scale as they enter.

FORM: Brief-pinned by the user — Apple AirPods Pro page reference plus a Canvas Scroll Clip image sequence. No concept roll (a pinned direction beats the roll).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
